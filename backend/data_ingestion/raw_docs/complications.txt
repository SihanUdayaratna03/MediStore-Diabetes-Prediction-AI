# Diabetes Complications — Clinical Reference

## Microvascular Complications
- Diabetic Retinopathy: leading cause of new blindness in adults
  - Annual dilated eye exam recommended
  - HbA1c control reduces risk by 76%
- Diabetic Nephropathy: progressive kidney disease
  - Monitor: ACR (albumin-creatinine ratio) annually
  - Target: ACR < 30 mg/g
- Peripheral Neuropathy: affects 50% of diabetic patients
  - Symptoms: tingling, numbness, pain in feet
  - Annual foot exam critical

## Macrovascular Complications
- Cardiovascular disease: 2–4× higher risk in T2D
  - Aspirin therapy for high-risk patients
  - Statin therapy: LDL target < 70 mg/dL
- Stroke risk: increased 2–4× in diabetic patients
- Peripheral arterial disease: affects 20–30% of T2D patients

## Prevention Strategies
- Tight glycaemic control (HbA1c < 7%)
- BP control < 130/80 mmHg
- Lipid management: statins + lifestyle
- Regular screening and monitoring

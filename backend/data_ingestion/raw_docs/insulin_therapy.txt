# Insulin Therapy Guidelines

## When to Initiate Insulin
- Type 1 Diabetes: required from diagnosis
- Type 2: HbA1c > 10%, or failure of 2–3 oral agents
- Acute illness or hospitalisation requiring immediate control

## Insulin Types
- Rapid-acting (Aspart, Lispro): onset 15 min, peak 1–2hr
- Short-acting (Regular): onset 30–60 min, peak 2–4hr
- Intermediate (NPH): onset 2–4hr, peak 4–12hr
- Long-acting (Glargine, Detemir): onset 2–4hr, no peak
- Ultra-long (Degludec): 42hr duration, minimal variability

## Starting Doses
- Basal insulin: 10 units/day or 0.1–0.2 units/kg/day
- Titrate: increase by 2 units every 3 days until fasting BG < 130 mg/dL

## Hypoglycaemia Management
- Mild/moderate: 15g fast-acting carbohydrate (glucose tablets)
- Severe: glucagon injection or IV dextrose
- Target: avoid BG < 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L)

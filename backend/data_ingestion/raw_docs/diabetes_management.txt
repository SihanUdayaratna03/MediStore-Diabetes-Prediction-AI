# ADA Diabetes Management Guidelines 2024

## Diagnosis Criteria
- Fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L)
- 2-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during OGTT
- HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
- Random plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with symptoms

## Blood Glucose Targets
- Preprandial: 80–130 mg/dL
- Postprandial (1–2 hr): <180 mg/dL
- HbA1c target: <7.0% for most adults

## Lifestyle Modifications
- Weight loss of 5–7% body weight reduces progression by 58%
- 150 min/week moderate aerobic activity recommended
- Low glycaemic index diet reduces HbA1c by 0.5%

## Pharmacological Treatment
- First-line: Metformin (if not contraindicated)
- Second-line: GLP-1 receptor agonists, SGLT2 inhibitors
- Insulin therapy for Type 1; may be required in Type 2

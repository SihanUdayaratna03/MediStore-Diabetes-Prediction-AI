# Nutritional Management of Diabetes

## Carbohydrate Management
- Total carbohydrate: 45–60g per meal
- Prefer complex carbs: whole grains, legumes, vegetables
- Limit refined sugars, sugary drinks, processed foods
- Glycaemic Index: choose low-GI foods (< 55)

## Macronutrient Targets
- Protein: 15–20% of total calories; prioritise lean sources
- Fat: < 35% of calories; saturated fat < 10%; avoid trans fats
- Fibre: 25–38g/day; slows glucose absorption

## Beneficial Dietary Patterns
- Mediterranean diet: reduces HbA1c by 0.3–0.5%
- DASH diet: reduces blood pressure; good for T2D with hypertension
- Low-carbohydrate diet: can reduce HbA1c by 0.5–1.0%

## Foods to Emphasise
- Non-starchy vegetables: unlimited
- Whole grains: oats, barley, quinoa
- Lean protein: fish (especially oily fish 2×/week), poultry, legumes
- Healthy fats: olive oil, avocado, nuts (1 oz/day)

## Foods to Limit
- Added sugars: < 25g/day (WHO recommendation)
- Refined white carbs: white bread, white rice, pastries
- Saturated fats: red meat, full-fat dairy
- Alcohol: no more than 1 drink/day (women) or 2 (men)

# Diabetes Risk Factors — Clinical Reference

## Primary Risk Factors
- BMI > 25 (overweight) or > 30 (obese) significantly increases T2D risk
- Age > 45: risk doubles every decade after 40
- Family history: first-degree relative with T2D
- Fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL (prediabetes)
- Gestational diabetes history

## Metabolic Risk Markers
- HbA1c 5.7–6.4%: prediabetes range
- Triglycerides > 150 mg/dL: metabolic syndrome marker
- HDL < 40 mg/dL (men) or < 50 mg/dL (women)
- Blood pressure ≥ 130/80 mmHg

## Lifestyle Risk Factors
- Physical inactivity: 2× increased risk
- Poor sleep (< 6hr/night): increases insulin resistance
- Chronic stress: elevates cortisol, raises blood glucose
- High refined carbohydrate diet: spikes postprandial glucose
